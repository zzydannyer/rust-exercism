# Instructions

# 指示

Given a target word and one or more candidate words, your task is to find the candidates that are anagrams of the target.

给定一个目标词和一个或多个候选词，您的任务是找到目标词的字谜词的候选词。

An anagram is a rearrangement of letters to form a new word: for example `"owns"` is an anagram of `"snow"`.
A word is _not_ its own anagram: for example, `"stop"` is not an anagram of `"stop"`.

字谜词是字母重新排列以形成新单词：例如 `"owns"` 是 `"snow"` 的字谜词。
一个单词_不是_它自己的字谜：例如，`"stop"` 不是 `"stop"` 的字谜。

The target word and candidate words are made up of one or more ASCII alphabetic characters (`A`-`Z` and `a`-`z`).
Lowercase and uppercase characters are equivalent: for example, `"PoTS"` is an anagram of `"sTOp"`, but `"StoP"` is not an anagram of `"sTOp"`.
The words you need to find should be taken from the candidate words, using the same letter case.

目标词和候选词由一个或多个ASCII字母字符（`A`-`Z`和`a`-`z`）组成。
小写和大写字符是等效的：例如，`"PoTS"` 是 `"sTOp"` 的字谜词，但 `"StoP"` 不是 `"sTOp"` 的字谜词。
您需要查找的单词应取自候选单词，并使用相同的字母大小写。

Given the target `"stone"` and the candidate words `"stone"`, `"tones"`, `"banana"`, `"tons"`, `"notes"`, and `"Seton"`, the anagram words you need to find are `"tones"`, `"notes"`, and `"Seton"`.


给定目标 `"stone"` 和候选词 `"stone"`、`"tones"`、`"banana"`、`"tons"`、`"notes"` 和 `"Seton"`，您需要查找的字谜词是 `"tones"`、`"notes"` 和ZXQ保护9QXZ。
