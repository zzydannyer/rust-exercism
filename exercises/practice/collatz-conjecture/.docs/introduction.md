# 介绍

One evening, you stumbled upon an old notebook filled with cryptic scribbles, as though someone had been obsessively chasing an idea.
On one page, a single question stood out: **Can every number find its way to 1?**
It was tied to something called the **Collatz Conjecture**, a puzzle that has baffled thinkers for decades.

一天晚上，你偶然发现一本旧笔记本，上面写满了神秘的涂鸦，就好像有人一直在痴迷地追逐一个想法。
在一页上，有一个问题很突出：**每个数字都能找到 1 的方法吗？**
它与“科拉茨猜想”有关，这是一个困扰思想家数十年的谜题。

The rules were deceptively simple.
Pick any positive integer.

规则看似简单。
选择任意正整数。

- If it's even, divide it by 2.
- If it's odd, multiply it by 3 and add 1.

- 如果是偶数，则除以 2。
- 如果是奇数，则乘以 3 加 1。

Then, repeat these steps with the result, continuing indefinitely.

然后，根据结果重复这些步骤，无限期地继续下去。

Curious, you picked number 12 to test and began the journey:

出于好奇，您选择了 12 号进行测试并开始了旅程：

12 ➜ 6 ➜ 3 ➜ 10 ➜ 5 ➜ 16 ➜ 8 ➜ 4 ➜ 2 ➜ 1

Counting from the second number (6), it took 9 steps to reach 1, and each time the rules repeated, the number kept changing.
At first, the sequence seemed unpredictable — jumping up, down, and all over.
Yet, the conjecture claims that no matter the starting number, we'll always end at 1.

从第二个数字（6）算起，需要9步才能达到1，并且每重复一次规则，数字就不断变化。
起初，这个顺序似乎不可预测——跳上跳下，然后到处跳。
然而，该猜想声称，无论起始数字如何，我们都将始终以 1 结束。

It was fascinating, but also puzzling.
Why does this always seem to work?
Could there be a number where the process breaks down, looping forever or escaping into infinity?
The notebook suggested solving this could reveal something profound — and with it, fame, [fortune][collatz-prize], and a place in history awaits whoever could unlock its secrets.

这很令人着迷，但也令人困惑。
为什么这似乎总是有效？
是否存在某个数字导致该过程崩溃、永远循环或逃逸到无穷大？
笔记本表明，解决这个问题可能会揭示一些深刻的东西——随之而来的是名誉、[财富][collat​​z-prize]，以及在历史上的一席之地等待着任何能够解开其秘密的人。
