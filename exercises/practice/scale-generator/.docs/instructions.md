Scales in Western music are based on the chromatic (12-note) scale. This
scale can be expressed as the following group of pitches:

西方音乐中的音阶是基于半音阶（12音符）音阶的。这个
音阶可以表示为以下一组音高：

A, A#, B, C, C#, D, D#, E, F, F#, G, G#

A given sharp note (indicated by a #) can also be expressed as the flat
of the note above it (indicated by a b) so the chromatic scale can also be
written like this:

给定的升号（用 # 表示）也可以表示为降号
其上方的音符（由 a b 表示），因此半音阶也可以是
像这样写：

A, Bb, B, C, Db, D, Eb, E, F, Gb, G, Ab

The major and minor scale and modes are subsets of this twelve-pitch
collection. They have seven pitches, and are called diatonic scales.

大调和小调音阶和调式是这十二个音高的子集
收藏。它们有七个音高，称为全音阶。
这些音阶中的音符集合是用升号或升号书写的
平底鞋，取决于补品。以下是其中的列表：

No Sharps or Flats:

a minor

没有升音或平音：

使用锐器：

The diatonic scales, and all other scales that derive from the
chromatic scale, are built upon intervals. An interval is the space
between two pitches.

全音阶以及所有其他衍生自的音阶
半音阶是建立在音程基础上的。间隔就是空间
两个球场之间。

The simplest interval is between two adjacent notes, and is called a
"half step", or "minor second" (sometimes written as a lower-case "m").
The interval between two notes that have an interceding note is called
a "whole step" or "major second" (written as an upper-case "M"). The
diatonic scales are built using only these two intervals between
adjacent notes.

最简单的音程是两个相邻音符之间的音程，称为
“半步”或“小秒”（有时写为小写“m”）。
有中间音符的两个音符之间的音程称为
“全音”或“大二度”（写为大写“M”）。的
全音阶音阶仅使用这两个音程来构建
相邻的注释。

Non-diatonic scales can contain other intervals.  An "augmented second"
interval, written "A", has two interceding notes (e.g., from A to C or Db to E)
or a "whole step" plus a "half step". There are also smaller and larger
intervals, but they will not figure into this exercise.


非全音阶可以包含其他音程。  “增强的第二秒”
音程，写作“A”，有两个中间音符（例如，从 A 到 C 或 Db 到 E）
或“全步”加“半步”。还有更小的和更大的
间隔，但它们不会计入本练习中。
