# 介绍

You have stumbled upon a group of mathematicians who are also singer-songwriters.
They have written a song for each of their favorite numbers, and, as you can imagine, they have a lot of favorite numbers (like [0][zero] or [73][seventy-three] or [6174][kaprekars-constant]).

您偶然发现了一群数学家，同时也是创作型歌手。
他们为每个最喜欢的数字写了一首歌，并且，正如您可以想象的，他们有很多最喜欢的数字（例如 [0][零] 或 [73][七十三] 或 [6174][kaprekars-constant]）。

You are curious to hear the song for your favorite number, but with so many songs to wade through, finding the right song could take a while.
Fortunately, they have organized their songs in a playlist sorted by the title — which is simply the number that the song is about.

您很想听听您最喜欢的歌曲的歌曲，但有这么多歌曲需要费力地浏览，找到合适的歌曲可能需要一段时间。
幸运的是，他们将歌曲组织在一个按标题排序的播放列表中——标题只是歌曲的编号。

You realize that you can use a binary search algorithm to quickly find a song given the title.

您意识到可以使用二分搜索算法快速找到给定标题的歌曲。
