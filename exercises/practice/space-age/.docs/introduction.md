# Introduction

# 介绍

The year is 2525 and you've just embarked on a journey to visit all planets in the Solar System (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune).
The first stop is Mercury, where customs require you to fill out a form (bureaucracy is apparently _not_ Earth-specific).
As you hand over the form to the customs officer, they scrutinize it and frown.
"Do you _really_ expect me to believe you're just 50 years old?
You must be closer to 200 years old!"

今年是 2525 年，您刚刚踏上旅程，参观太阳系中的所有行星（水星、金星、地球、火星、木星、土星、天王星和海王星）。
第一站是水星，那里的海关要求你填写一份表格（官僚主义显然不是地球特有的）。
当您将表格交给海关官员时，他们会仔细检查并皱起眉头。
“你_真的_希望我相信你才 50 岁吗？
你现在已经快200岁了！”

Amused, you wait for the customs officer to start laughing, but they appear to be dead serious.
You realize that you've entered your age in _Earth years_, but the officer expected it in _Mercury years_!
As Mercury's orbital period around the sun is significantly shorter than Earth, you're actually a lot older in Mercury years.
After some quick calculations, you're able to provide your age in Mercury Years.
The customs officer smiles, satisfied, and waves you through.
You make a mental note to pre-calculate your planet-specific age _before_ future customs checks, to avoid such mix-ups.

你被逗乐了，等着海关官员开始大笑，但他们看起来很严肃。
你意识到你输入的年龄是_地球年_，但官员预计是_水星年_！
由于水星绕太阳公转的周期明显短于地球，因此在水星年里你的年龄实际上要大得多。
经过一些快速计算后，您可以提供以水星年为单位的年龄。
海关官员满意地微笑着，挥手让你通过。
您在心里记下在未来海关检查之前预先计算您的行星特定年龄，以避免此类混淆。
